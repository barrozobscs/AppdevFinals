Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy explains how PacePath handles information when you use the PacePath study hub.

Information PacePath handles

  • Account details such as your name, email address when provided, profile photo, and sign-in provider identifier.
  • Study materials you upload, extracted text, generated study guides and quizzes, quiz answers, and learning progress.
  • Basic session and technical information needed to operate sign-in and the website.

How the information is used

PacePath uses account details to create and secure your account. Study materials and learning activity are used to provide reviewers, quizzes, topic analysis, and roadmaps. When you request AI-generated study resources, extracted text from your material is sent to the OpenAI API to generate those resources.

Sign-in and service providers

If you choose Google or Facebook sign-in, that provider authenticates you and shares the profile information you authorize, such as your account identifier, name, profile image, and email address when available. PacePath does not receive your provider password.

PacePath is hosted on Railway and uses a MySQL database hosted with the application. OpenAI processes extracted study text when AI generation is requested. These providers handle information under their own policies and service terms.

Storage and retention

Account, study, and progress data is stored to provide the service. Uploaded files are stored with the application. To request deletion of your account and associated data, follow the instructions on our Data Deletion page.

Your choices and questions

You can choose whether to use Google or Facebook sign-in and whether to upload study materials. To ask a privacy question or request access, correction, or deletion of your information, email lancejacoblumantas@gmail.com. Do not include your password in your request.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version and its revision date will be posted on this page.
