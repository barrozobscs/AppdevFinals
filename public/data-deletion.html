Data Deletion Instructions

You may request deletion of your PacePath account and the information associated with it, including your profile, linked social sign-in, uploaded study materials, generated resources, and learning progress.

How to request deletion

  1. Email lancejacoblumantas@gmail.com with the subject “PacePath data deletion request.”
  2. Tell us the email address on your PacePath account. If Facebook did not provide an email, include your PacePath display name and whether you used Facebook or Google sign-in.
  3. We may ask for reasonable information to confirm that you own the account. Never send your password.

After we verify the request, we will delete the account and associated data from the active PacePath application and confirm when the request is completed. Information that must be retained to meet a legal obligation may be kept as required.
